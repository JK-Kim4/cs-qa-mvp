I completely understand — a missing paycheck is stressful, especially with bills due, and I want to resolve this as quickly as possible.

Let me look at your payroll record right now.

I can see your direct deposit is set up correctly in our system, and payroll shows a payment was processed on [date] for your normal amount. That means the issue is somewhere between our bank and yours.

A few quick questions:
- Which bank holds your account?
- Have you checked with your bank whether there's a pending deposit, or whether there's any account flag that might be holding the transfer?

In the meantime, here's what I'm doing:

**Step 1**: I'm flagging your case with our payroll team right now as urgent — they have a direct line to our payment processor and can trace exactly where the transfer is.

**Step 2**: Depending on what they find, there are two paths:
- If the transfer is pending at your bank: they can often expedite release with a trace number (I'll get that for you)
- If the transfer genuinely failed: the payroll team can initiate a same-day or next-day reissue, either as a new direct deposit or a check pickup from HR

**Step 3**: Because of the timing with your bills, I'm going to ask the payroll team lead to prioritize this — please give me until 2 PM today for an update. I'll call or email you directly.

Is there a best number or email to reach you this afternoon?

I know "we're looking into it" isn't what you need right now. I hear the urgency and I'll follow this personally until it's resolved.
