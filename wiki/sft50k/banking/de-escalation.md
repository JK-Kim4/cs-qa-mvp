## 대표 답변

> 이 주제에서 가장 전형적인 상담원 답변 (다른 답변들과 내용이 가장 많이 겹치는 답변)

I hear you, and I'm not going to defend what happened — four calls and an hour of hold time for a single issue is not acceptable, and I understand why you're angry.

My name is [name] and I'm a senior agent. I have your account in front of me right now. I'm not transferring you and I'm not putting you on hold.

Tell me what the issue is, and I'm going to fix it on this call — or tell you exactly why it can't be fixed instantly and what will happen by end of day.

You've been patient well past what any customer should have to be. I'm listening.
