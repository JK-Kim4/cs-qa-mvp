**If you need immediate account access today:**

Option 1: I can update your email address on file right now (identity verified) so the reset link goes to your current email. I'll then send a new reset link.

Option 2: Visit any Meridian branch with a valid photo ID — a banker can verify you in person and restore your access on the spot. This is the fastest route if you need access within the hour.

**For your urgent bills**: I can also process a one-time bill payment directly from your account over the phone right now, if you'd like — that way nothing goes late while we sort out the online access.
