## 대표 답변

> 이 주제에서 가장 전형적인 상담원 답변 (다른 답변들과 내용이 가장 많이 겹치는 답변)

I hear you, and I'm sorry — three contacts for the same unresolved issue is a failure on our part, not yours, and I understand why you're considering cancelling.

I'm a senior specialist and I'm not going to pass you to anyone else. I'm going to own this until it's fixed.

First: can you tell me what you've been told in the previous three contacts? I want to know what was already tried so I don't waste your time repeating it.

And while you're telling me that — can you share:
- What size data exports are timing out? (rows, or approximate file size)
- What error message do you see when it times out?
- Is this affecting all exports or specific ones?

I want to understand the full picture before I tell you anything. What I can tell you right now is: if this is a known platform issue affecting your specific data volume or export type, I will tell you honestly what the timeline for a fix is. If it's a configuration issue on your account, we'll fix it today. And if there's a workaround that gets you back to functional while a permanent fix is in progress, we'll do that too.

You shouldn't have had to contact us three times. Let's fix it now.
