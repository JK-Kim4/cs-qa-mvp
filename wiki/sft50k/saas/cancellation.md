Can I ask a quick question? You mentioned the price has gotten too high — is the main issue the monthly cost, the current feature set, or both?

I ask because depending on your situation, there may be a better option than cancellation:

**If it's primarily cost:**
- Our **Starter plan** is $29/month (vs. your current $79 Pro) — it includes 5 users, which works for most small teams. If your team is under 5, this might cover everything you actually need.
- If you're open to paying annually, I can offer **2 months free** right now, which brings your effective monthly cost down to $66/month on Pro.

**If you need to cancel entirely**, I'll take care of that for you right now — no friction, no retention forms. Your data will remain accessible through the end of your current billing period.

Which direction makes sense for you? And if you're open to it, it would genuinely help us to know what's driving the decision — even if you do cancel, that feedback directly shapes how we improve the product.
