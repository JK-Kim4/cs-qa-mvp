Completely valid feedback — dark mode is consistently one of our top-requested features, and I want to give you an honest answer.

**Current status**: Dark mode is on our product roadmap. I can't give you a specific release date (our team doesn't share public timelines until features are close to shipping), but I can tell you it's actively planned, not just "someday maybe."

**In the meantime**: Most browsers offer a "force dark mode" extension that applies a dark theme to all websites, which works reasonably well with CloudBase's interface. It's not perfect — some elements can look odd — but it's an option if the eye strain is significant for you.

I'll add your account to the feature request tracking for dark mode — when it ships, you'll receive a notification. Would you also like me to pass along any specific feedback about which parts of the interface are most difficult at night? That kind of detail helps our design team prioritize.
