## 대표 답변

> 이 주제에서 가장 전형적인 상담원 답변 (다른 답변들과 내용이 가장 많이 겹치는 답변)

Thank you for reaching out, and I want to acknowledge your loyalty — it genuinely matters to us, and I want to give you an honest and respectful answer.

The final sale designation means items are sold at a significant discount with the understanding that they can't be returned — this is shown clearly at checkout and on your order confirmation. I want to be straightforward with you: I'm not able to process a cash refund on a final sale item, and I want to be honest about that rather than give you the runaround.

That said, here's what I *can* do:

**If the item is defective** — a seam issue, a broken zipper, manufacturing damage — that's a completely different situation, and I can offer an exchange or store credit regardless of the sale designation. Is there any issue with the item itself?

**If it's a fit or style issue** — I can offer a **one-time exchange** for another size of the same item if it's still in stock, as a goodwill gesture for your loyalty history.

**What I can't do** is a cash refund for a change of mind on a clearly-marked final sale item — and I'd rather be honest about that than have you spend time on hold for an answer that won't change.

I do want to make sure you feel valued as a customer. Is there something specific that's driving the return request that I might be able to address another way?
