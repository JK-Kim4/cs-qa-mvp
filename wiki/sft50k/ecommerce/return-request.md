## 대표 답변

> 이 주제에서 가장 전형적인 상담원 답변 (다른 답변들과 내용이 가장 많이 겹치는 답변)

I appreciate you explaining the situation, and I understand that traveling can make it genuinely difficult to manage returns within the standard window.

Our policy is 30 days from delivery, so at 5 weeks we're just outside of it — but I don't want to just say "no" without looking at your full account and order history, because we do have some flexibility for good customers in exceptional circumstances.

Let me pull up your account...

I can see this is your third order with us over the past year, and this is the first time you've requested a return — so I'm happy to make an exception here.

Here's what I can offer:

**Option 1 — Store credit**: I can issue store credit for the full purchase price. This is the easiest route and doesn't require you to return the jacket immediately.

**Option 2 — Exchange**: If you'd like a different size, color, or item, I can set up an even exchange once you return the jacket.

For either option, I'd need the jacket to be unworn with the original tags attached. Can you confirm it's in that condition?

I'll also send you a prepaid return label so you won't have any shipping costs on your end.

Let me know which works better for you and we'll get this taken care of.
