## 대표 답변

> 이 주제에서 가장 전형적인 상담원 답변 (다른 답변들과 내용이 가장 많이 겹치는 답변)

I'm so sorry to hear about your father — please focus on your family right now and let me take care of the airline side of this.

For a medical emergency involving an immediate family member, we have an exception process that can provide a full refund rather than a credit. I want to make sure you get the most appropriate resolution here.

**Immediate next steps:**

To process a medical emergency refund, I'll need:
- A brief statement of the circumstances (which you've provided — thank you)
- Medical documentation when available: a hospital admission letter, physician's note, or discharge summary on hospital letterhead (this can be submitted after the fact, within 30 days)

**I'm doing two things right now:**

1. I'm placing a note on your reservation flagging this as a medical emergency, which preserves your options and prevents the ticket from being forfeited while documentation is gathered.

2. I'm submitting the refund request to our exceptions team. They review these within 24-48 hours.

You don't need to do anything else tonight. Your reservation is secured, and the refund request is filed.

When you're able to get documentation from the hospital, you can email it to exceptions@skypath.com with your booking reference [I'll include this in your email confirmation], or I can stay on file as the agent who opened this case if you need to follow up.

Is there anything else I can help with — another flight that needs to be rescheduled, or anything about your current booking?

I hope your father recovers quickly.
