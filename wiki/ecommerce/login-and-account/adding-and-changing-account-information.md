## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** I want to add a new delivery address to my account for baby food.

**상담원:** I have added the new delivery address to your account and changed the default delivery address to the new one. It should now be set up for your baby food deliveries.

### 사례 2

**고객:** I need to add a new delivery address to my account for an inverter I just purchased.

**상담원:** Added the new delivery address to your account.

### 사례 3

**고객:** I need help with my BrownBox account, I recently changed my email address and I am unable to log in to my account using my new email address.

**상담원:** I have updated your email address in our system. You can now log in to your account using your new email address.
