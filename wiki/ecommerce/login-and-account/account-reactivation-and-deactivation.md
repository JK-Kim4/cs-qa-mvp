Your account was deactivated due to inactivity, but I have reactivated it for you. You can log in now.

## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** Can you help me reactivate my account?

**상담원:** To reactivate your account, I need to verify your information. Can you provide me with your billing address and phone number associated with your account?

### 사례 2

**고객:** How can I reactivate my account for a wristwatch that I purchased from BrownBox website?

**상담원:** To reactivate your account, you need to provide your email address, order number, full name, and phone number. You will be transferred to the account reactivation team for further assistance.

### 사례 3

**고객:** What assistance does the customer need?

**상담원:** The customer needs to deactivate their account for a Bp monitor and cancel their subscription.
