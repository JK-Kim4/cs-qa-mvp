## 대표 답변

> 이 주제에서 가장 전형적인 상담원 답변 (다른 답변들과 내용이 가장 많이 겹치는 답변)

In order to ensure the security of your account and to prevent unauthorized access, you need to verify your mobile number and email address. You will receive a verification code on your mobile number and email address. Enter the verification code in the respective fields and click on the verify button to complete the verification process.

**고객:** Why is the system asking me to verify my mobile number or email address?

**상담원:** The verification requirement is a security measure to ensure that only authorized users can access your account. It's a one-time process, and it helps protect your personal information.

**상담원:** To change the email ID linked to your BP monitor order, I need to verify your mobile number first. I will send a verification code to your mobile number. After you enter the verification code on the website, I can update your email ID in our system.
