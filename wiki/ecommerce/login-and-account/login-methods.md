## 대표 답변

> 이 주제에서 가장 전형적인 상담원 답변 (다른 답변들과 내용이 가장 많이 겹치는 답변)

You cannot log in to your BrownBox account on the mobile app using your mobile number because you have registered with your email address. You can log in using your email and password.

## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** I am having trouble logging into my account on the BrownBox mobile app for my laptop. I want to log in with my mobile number, but it's not working.

**상담원:** Your mobile number is not linked to your account. I have guided you through linking your mobile number to your account, and now you can log in using your mobile number.

### 사례 2

**고객:** I am having trouble using my corporate email ID for the sign-up process

**상담원:** Due to system limitations, the corporate email ID 'john@abc.com' cannot be used for registration. I have registered your personal email ID 'johnsmith@gmail.com' with our system. You can now use it to sign up for the air cooler.

### 사례 3

**고객:** I am having trouble logging into my BrownBox account on the mobile app using my mobile number.

**상담원:** After checking the account, it seems that the mobile number is not registered with us. I suggested uninstalling and reinstalling the app and trying to log in again. If the issue persists, I recommended logging in with the email address instead of the mobile number.
