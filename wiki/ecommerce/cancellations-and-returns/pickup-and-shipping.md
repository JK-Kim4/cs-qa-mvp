## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** Why do I need to ship the backpack I purchased from BrownBox?

**상담원:** The backpack is part of a recall due to a defect that could potentially cause harm. You need to return it for a full refund.

### 사례 2

**고객:** What is the issue with the customer's refund for the returned coffee maker?

**상담원:** The agent acknowledged the customer's issue and escalated it to the finance team for further investigation.

### 사례 3

**고객:** How can I change the address for my power bank order?

**상담원:** I have contacted the courier and arranged for the customer to pick up the item from their nearest office located at 789 Main Street, Anytown, USA. I also provided the customer with the necessary details for the pick up.
