### 사례 2

**상담원:** John helped the customer, Mark, cancel his order for a ceiling fan that was still in processing status. The cancellation process was expedited and was expected to be completed within 12 hours. John also confirmed that Mark would receive an email confirmation of the cancellation.

### 사례 3

**고객:** How can I cancel my order BB123456 because I found the same pair of shoes at a lower price from another retailer?

**상담원:** I have cancelled your order BB123456 for you. It usually takes 2-3 business days for the refund to reflect on your account. Please note that we process orders quickly, so we had to check if your order had already been shipped before cancelling it.
