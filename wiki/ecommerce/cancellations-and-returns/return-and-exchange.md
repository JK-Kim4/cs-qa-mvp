### 사례 2

**고객:** What should I do to return and exchange a defective DSLR camera that I purchased from BrownBox's website?

**상담원:** To return and exchange the defective DSLR camera, the customer needs to initiate a return shipping or pickup. However, since there is no reverse pickup facility in the customer's area, the customer has to ship the camera back to BrownBox. The customer is not eligible for a full refund of the shipping charges, but BrownBox offers a partial refund of Rs. 200 and a discount coupon of Rs. 500 for the next purchase as a gesture of goodwill. Once BrownBox receives the camera, they will initiate the exchange process and dispatch the replacement camera within 3-5 business days.
