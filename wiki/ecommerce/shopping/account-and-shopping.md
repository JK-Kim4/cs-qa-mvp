## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** Can you help me understand what the hidden charges on my bill are for?

**상담원:** The hidden charges on your bill are an additional shipping fee of $10.99 for shipping to a remote location. This fee cannot be waived, but free shipping is offered on orders over $50.

### 사례 2

**고객:** Why are there hidden charges for my recent purchase of a hand blender from BrownBox?

**상담원:** The billing team agent apologized for the inconvenience and mentioned that the additional charges for shipping and handling were not mentioned on the checkout page. They assured the customer that they would refund the additional charges to their account.

### 사례 3

**고객:** I recently purchased a water purifier from BrownBox and noticed an unexpected additional charge on my credit card statement. Can you help me with this?

**상담원:** The additional charge was for the installation of the water purifier, which was mentioned in the product description. Since the customer wasn't aware of it, I offered a discount code for their next purchase instead of refunding the installation charges.
