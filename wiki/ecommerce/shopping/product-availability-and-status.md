## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** What is the problem I am facing?

**상담원:** The problem is that Tom cannot find the international shipping option for a specific sweatshirt on the BrownBox website.

### 사례 2

**고객:** Why can't I buy a BrownBox Backpack and ship it to Australia?

**상담원:** The BrownBox Backpack is not available for international shipping, including Australia. This is due to several factors such as customs regulations and shipping costs. However, there are similar products available for international shipping, such as the BlackBox Backpack and the GreyBox Backpack.

### 사례 3

**고객:** What is the status of my order (BB987654321) for a BP monitor?

**상담원:** The BP monitor that Alex ordered is currently out of stock, and the estimated delivery time for the next shipment is within two weeks. Since Alex needs the BP monitor urgently, the agent offered international express shipping for an additional fee of $50, with an estimated delivery time of 5-7 business days.
