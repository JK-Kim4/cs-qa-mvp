## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** I am having an issue with the exchange offer for a BP monitor and not getting the promised discount on the website.

**상담원:** A technical glitch caused the discount to not reflect in the order. The agent arranged for the discount to be applied and processed $30 off, which should reflect in the order within 24 hours.

### 사례 2

**고객:** Why are the same T-Shirt priced differently on different pages?

**상담원:** The price difference is due to different discount coupons offered on different pages.

### 사례 3

**고객:** Can you tell me more about the instant cashback offer on Oven Toaster Grills (OTG) on BrownBox's website?

I provided information about the 10% cashback offer and helped process the purchase, applying the discount. The final price after the discount was $89.99.
