The minimum order value for free delivery is $50, and your order's value is $35. That's why you were charged a delivery fee. You can add more items to your cart to meet the minimum order value requirement or subscribe to our loyalty program, which offers free delivery for all orders, regardless of their value.

## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** Why was I charged for shipping for my Pendrive order?

**상담원:** You were charged for shipping because the total amount of your order was $15, and the free delivery qualification is for orders above $25.

### 사례 2

**고객:** Why was I charged for delivery on my recent headphone order?

**상담원:** You were charged for delivery because the headphone you ordered was priced at $20, and the minimum order value for free delivery is $25. If you had added another item to your order worth $5 or more, you would have qualified for free delivery.

### 사례 3

**고객:** Can you tell me why I didn't qualify for free delivery for my refrigerator order?

**상담원:** The refrigerator you ordered is large and heavy, which exceeds the weight and size limit for free delivery. You can consider other delivery options like standard, expedited, or white glove delivery for an additional fee, which includes delivery, installation, and removal of your old appliance.
