## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** Can you help me order a Seiko wristwatch?

**상담원:** The Seiko wristwatch is currently not available at the customer's location or online, and the agent was unable to find it in stock at any other location. The supervisor also confirmed the unavailability of the product, but offered a discount on any other product purchased from them.

### 사례 2

### 사례 3

**고객:** Why is the Samsung 4-Door Flex Refrigerator not available for shipping to my location?

**상담원:** Due to logistical limitations such as the product's weight, size, and the distance between the warehouse and the delivery location, the Samsung 4-Door Flex Refrigerator is not available for shipping to the customer's location. The customer was offered alternative products, but they insisted on this specific model. The agent did not have the authority to override the system's restrictions, but a senior agent was able to escalate the issue and check for any exceptions. However, the override was not possible at the moment, and the customer was kept updated about the possibility of future availability.
