## 개요

- 출처: `ecommerce` (MIT)
- 분류: Order / Order Delivery Issues

## 대표 답변

> 이 주제에서 가장 전형적인 상담원 답변 (다른 답변들과 내용이 가장 많이 겹치는 답변)

The order is currently delayed due to unforeseen shipping circumstances. However, it has been escalated to the shipping team and the customer can expect the delivery within the next 2-3 business days. Additionally, a 10% discount has been offered on the customer's next purchase as an apology for the inconvenience caused.

## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** What happened to my order of Pendrive that was out for delivery two days ago?

**상담원:** The order was delayed due to unforeseen circumstances, but a similar Pendrive will be shipped to the customer from a nearby warehouse and received by tomorrow.

### 사례 2

**고객:** What is the status of my refrigerator delivery, which was supposed to be delivered last week?

**상담원:** The refrigerator is still in transit and hasn't arrived at the local delivery center yet. However, I have contacted the delivery team and confirmed that it will be delivered within the next 48 hours, on Thursday between 10 am and 2 pm. Additionally, I have applied a $50 credit to your account for the inconvenience.

### 사례 3

**고객:** What can I do to track my order for an external hard disk?

**상담원:** You can contact our courier partner, XYZ Couriers, at 1-800-XYZ-1234 to get the delivery status of your order. I have applied a 10% discount to your account as a token of appreciation for your patience.
