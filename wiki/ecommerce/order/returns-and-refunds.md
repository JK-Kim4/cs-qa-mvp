### 사례 1

**고객:** What is the problem with the microwave oven that the customer received?

### 사례 2

**고객:** I want to return a T-Shirt that doesn't fit me and get a refund.

**상담원:** Initiated the return process for the T-Shirt and confirmed a refund will be issued within 7-10 business days once the seller receives and verifies the return.

### 사례 3

**고객:** I need to return a damaged BP monitor that I purchased from BrownBox's website.

**상담원:** The customer needs to follow the seller's return policy, which allows returning the product within 30 days of purchase, as long as it's unused and the packaging is intact. The customer is responsible for return shipping costs. Here are the steps to initiate the return process: 1. Log in to the BrownBox account, go to 'My Orders', and select the order. 2. Click on 'Return Item' and follow the instructions. 3. After submitting the return request, an email with the return label and instructions will be sent. 4. Pack the item securely, attach the return label, and ship it back. Once the item is received, the refund will be processed within 3-5 business days.
