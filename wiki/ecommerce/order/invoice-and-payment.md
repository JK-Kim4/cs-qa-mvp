## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** I, Emily, need an invoice for my recent purchase from BrownBox because I was not given one and I require it for reimbursement from my company.

**상담원:** Sarah, the agent, acknowledged the issue and after obtaining Emily's email and order number, she discovered a system error that prevented the invoice from being generated. Sarah then informed Emily that she would send the invoice to her email address. The solution was implemented when Sarah sent the invoice to Emily.

### 사례 2

**고객:** Can the agent help me get the invoice for my recent laptop purchase?

**상담원:** The agent helped the customer get the invoice for their recent laptop purchase and confirmed the payment method used for the order.

### 사례 3

**고객:** Why is 'Cash on Delivery' not available for the blue shirt with the white collar and the product code BB12345678?

**상담원:** There was an error in our system that mistakenly disabled 'Cash on Delivery' for the mentioned shirt. I have enabled it, and you should be able to select it while placing your order.
