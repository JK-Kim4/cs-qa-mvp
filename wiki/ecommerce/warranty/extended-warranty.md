To sign up for an extended warranty, log into your account on our website, go to the 'My Orders' section, select the order containing the item for which you want to sign up for the extended warranty, and click on the 'Extended Warranty' button. Follow the instructions on the screen to complete the process. The cost of a one-year extended warranty for your product is $19.99.

### 사례 2

**고객:** How can I sign up for an extended warranty for the mobile I purchased from BrownBox?

**상담원:** To sign up for an extended warranty, visit the BrownBox website, click on the 'Extended Warranty' tab, select the product, and follow the prompts. The extended warranty can be purchased within 30 days of the original purchase date, covers accidental and liquid damage, and can only be purchased for products still under the manufacturer's warranty. The cost varies depending on the product and length of the warranty.
