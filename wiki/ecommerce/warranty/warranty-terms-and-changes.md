## 답변 사례

### 사례 1

**고객:** What is the impact of the warranty terms change on Jane's ceiling fan?

**상담원:** The changes in warranty terms do not impact Jane's product's warranty since she purchased it before the changes were made. The new warranty terms will only apply to products purchased after the effective date of the changes.

### 사례 2

**고객:** What is the impact of the recent changes in warranty terms for the Induction Cooktop with order number BB123456789?

**상담원:** The recent changes in warranty terms for the Induction Cooktop with order number BB123456789 will not impact the product's warranty. The changes include an extended warranty period, improved coverage for damages, and better customer support services.

### 사례 3

**고객:** What is the warranty period for the electric kettle I purchased from BrownBox?

**상담원:** The warranty period for your electric kettle is 1 year from the date of purchase.
